---
name: Funding display conventions
description: User-required sign colours for funding rates, distinct from LSAR changes.
---
On Funding Rate screens, funding above zero is red and funding below zero is green.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested the opposite of the original colour mapping.

**How to apply:** retain this convention in Funding tables, aggregated values,
summary cards and highest/lowest rankings, including future funding views.
Keep it scoped to Funding rather than reversing LSAR colours or exchange-status
signals.

For rates quoted at an eight-hour interval or with an unknown interval, show only the coloured funding
percentage, without a duplicate native-rate or interval label.

**Why:** the user requested no “Native” or “8h” text alongside eight-hour rates,
then requested no extra text for unknown intervals either.

**How to apply:** use this for current exchange rates and highest/lowest entries.
Keep native-rate and interval explanations only for known non-eight-hour
intervals, where the exchange quote differs from the comparison basis.
